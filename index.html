what H.I.M. is

H.I.M. ("Hi, It's Me") is a community-first space for LGBTQ+ people to make friends and stay in touch.

It brings back the mechanics that made the early internet feel personal — not for nostalgia, but because they made more room for identity, regular interaction, and actual community.

what's inside
01
your screenname

Pick a name that represents you. Set a status. Let your buddies see when you're around.

02
chat rooms

Community rooms organized by city and interest. See who's around, jump in, and add people to your buddy list when you click.

03
buddy list + away messages

A list of the people you actually talk to. Set an away message — "grabbing coffee," "heads-down at work" — status as self-expression, the way it used to be.

04
direct messages

Private one-to-one conversations with your buddies. Text, photos, and voice notes. Messages queue offline and send when you're back.
